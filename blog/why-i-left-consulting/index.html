Why I left consulting to build products

April 10, 2026

After years of advising companies on how to run better systems, I realized I wanted to stop explaining what to build and start building it myself. Here's what pushed me over the edge.

Consulting taught me how to spot repeated pain: handoffs that disappear, reports that age out, and teams rebuilding the same workflow every quarter.

The shift was simple: I wanted the recommendation to become a product. That means owning the rough edges, shipping the first version, and learning from actual usage instead of a slide review.

This site is the lab for that habit. Every post, backend hook, and subscription flow gives me another rep building durable software in public.
